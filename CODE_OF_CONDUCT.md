# Code of conduct

Arca Verborum follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful of contributors and of the communities whose languages are
recorded here. Credit sources and people accurately, and disagree about
evidence, not about persons.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, Tiago Tresoldi
(contact: TO BE ADDED). Reports are handled confidentially.
